@vibe/engine samples

Each page is also a measurement scene: add ?bench=1&size=1280x720 and use window.__sample from the console. Add ?quality=desktop or ?quality=mobile to force a graphics tier (the gear button picks one and remembers it).

Night NYC walkA walk through Manhattan after dark: buildings, street lamps, signs and night lighting. Miami driveA drive along the Miami waterfront: assets, driving feel and vehicle physics. Asset libraryEvery asset in the repository, downloaded or made in code: credits, 3D previews and the code to use each one. Midnight Candy RunHalloween 2026 game: collect candy before midnight, smash lanterns, BOO the cloth ghosts. Plays on phones (touch) and desktop.

Starters

Small, complete games to copy: one main.js each (about 150 lines), public engine imports only.

Starter: walkA third-person walker: animated GLB character, controller, camera rig, textured ground, a lamp, a HUD readout and a sound. Starter: driveA box car on the vehicle controller (an ordinary road car), chase camera, a textured road and a speed readout.

# Texture credits

Poly Haven texture sets, CC0 1.0 (<https://polyhaven.com>, <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>),
re-encoded JPEGs. The `.ktx2` files next to them are GPU-compressed (Basis Universal) copies written by
`tools/compress-textures.mjs` with the settings of miami-drive's sets (ETC1S q160 albedo / rough / AO, UASTC + Zstd
normals); the JPGs stay as the fallback (`?ktx2=0`). The transcoder is in `../basis/`. Per-set authors, sizes and
sources: ../CREDITS.md.
